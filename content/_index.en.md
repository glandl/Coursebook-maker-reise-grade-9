Welcome to the **Maker Journey**: in one school year (2 lessons per week) you will complete five projects covering all competencies of the Informatics curriculum for grades 9–11.

## The five projects

1. **Code & Gadget** – Python basics and your first interactive device
2. **Weather Station / IoT** – sensors, networks and data
3. **AI Lab** – from decision trees to generative AI
4. **Data Detectives** – SQL, encryption, privacy
5. **Web Portfolio** – your own project website, developed with agile methods

Along the way you keep a **learning journal** and work in teams using **Kanban and sprints**.
